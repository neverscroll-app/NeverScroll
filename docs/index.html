ONE VIDEO IS ENOUGH SWIPESTOPPED

Open. Watch. Leave.

Protection starts on a recognized short-video screen. There is no video count or timer to manage.

01

Open a link

Follow a short-video link from your messenger as usual.

02

Watch the video

Feed drags are blocked. You can still seek on a recognized playback bar and use short taps, such as pause or comments.

03

Go back

An exit action stays visible at the top. In TikTok it opens Recent apps; elsewhere it uses Back.

Where it works

Choose which apps to protect. Recognition is deliberately conservative so ordinary screens remain usable. Changes to another app’s interface can cause a video to be missed.

  • YouTubeShorts
  • YouTube ReVancedShorts from a direct link
  • InstagramReels
  • TikTokFor You feed

Your screen stays on your phone.

NeverScroll uses Android accessibility access to read visible interface elements, including playback controls, in selected apps and place a transparent layer over recognized feeds. It does not take screenshots, keep viewing history, use analytics or send data anywhere. Settings stay on your device.

If detection misses a video, an optional compatibility switch can block drags on every screen of that app, including messages and its regular feed. Recognized playback bars still allow horizontal seeking. It is off by default.

Set it up once.

Install the APK, open NeverScroll and enable its accessibility service after reading the in-app disclosure. You can turn protection off at any time.

Get the latest APK ↗

Available in English and Russian. No account or internet connection required by the app.

  1. Download and install the latest release APK.
  2. Open NeverScroll and allow “NeverScroll — Feed protection” in Android accessibility settings.
  3. Choose YouTube, YouTube ReVanced, Instagram or TikTok on the home screen.
